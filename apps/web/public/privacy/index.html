Privacy Policy

Purrkour itself does not collect, store, or share any personal data. There are no accounts and no analytics. The browser version shows no advertising and does no third-party tracking of any kind.

The iOS and Android apps can show an optional rewarded ad when you ask for a hint. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which may process device identifiers, IP address, and ad-interaction data to serve and measure ads — see Google's privacy policy. Where required, the app asks for your consent first (and on iOS, for tracking permission); if you decline, requests are limited to non-personalized ads.

Your game progress and settings are saved only on your own device: on iOS and Android in the app's local storage, in the browser in your browser's local storage. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you delete the app or clear your browser data.
